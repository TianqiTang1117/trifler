Every useful idea begins somewhere: a question, an experiment, a conversation,
or a problem that takes longer to understand than expected.

This site is a place to keep those beginnings and follow where they lead.

## What you'll find here

- **Notes** — ideas and explanations worth keeping.
- **Reflections** — lessons, observations, and changes in perspective.
- **Technical explorations** — practical details, worked examples, and things
  learned by building and testing.

The aim is to make learning easier to revisit and useful to share.
Entries can grow as understanding improves.

*A wanderer in the ocean of knowledge.*
